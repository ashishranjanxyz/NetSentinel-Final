I picked a lane, realized it wasn't mine, and built a new one — from scratch.

It started with AI and Machine Learning. I was obsessed — building models, processing data, making machines learn things humans take for granted. Then one question stopped me cold:

"What's the point of building intelligent systems if they're not secure?"

That question changed everything. I pivoted — hard — into Cybersecurity. Ranked in the Top 1% on TryHackMe India, solved 700+ rooms, earned 68+ badges, and made it to the Wall of Fame. Not by luck. By obsession.

The result? NetSentinel — an AI-powered network vulnerability scanner built from scratch. 20 modules. Real-world impact. And this is just the beginning.
